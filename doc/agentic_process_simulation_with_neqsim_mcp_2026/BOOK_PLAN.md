# Book plan

## Purpose

Show how agents coordinate databases, engineering applications and NeqSim
inside an oil and gas study. Explain each tool's contribution, the artifact
passed to the next tool, the check applied and the decision it supports.

## Current structure

The manifest defines ten chapters. Directory prefixes are historical names;
displayed chapter and section numbers follow the manifest.

| Chapter | Role in the tool-cooperation narrative |
|---|---|
| 1 | Establish the industrial question, evidence and decision |
| 2 | Discover, validate and invoke tools through MCP |
| 3 | Coordinate specialists through shared study state |
| 4 | Retrieve, reconcile and qualify heterogeneous industrial records |
| 5 | Connect modular process models to equipment and specialist tools |
| 6 | Carry controlled scenarios and evidence into safety studies |
| 7 | Combine tools in routine operational investigations |
| 8 | Separate advisory work, engineering approval and execution |
| 9 | Trace the complete worked study and its evidence handoffs |
| 10 | Reuse portable tool-chain playbooks across facilities |

## Revision scope

- Use public commercial products and open standards as category examples.
- Exclude descriptions of internal operator tools and databases.
- Add one original teaching diagram per chapter and an industrial scene.
- Add a coordinated illustrated cover.
- Verify calculable examples against the selected local NeqSim source.
- Check arithmetic, units, current interfaces, source citations and cross-references.
- Distinguish hypothetical teaching values, executed model outputs and independent validation.
- Rebuild and visually review HTML, PDF and editable book formats.

The page count follows the necessary explanation and legible figures; the
obsolete thirteen-chapter page budget does not govern this revision.
