## About the Author

Even Solbraa is a professor in process engineering and the original creator of NeqSim, a Java library for thermodynamic calculations and process simulation. His work has focused on applying rigorous thermodynamics, process modelling, and digital
engineering workflows to oil and gas production, gas processing, CCS, and field
development studies. This book reflects the ongoing effort to connect NeqSim
with modern agentic engineering workflows, including MCP tools, AI coding
agents, technical document retrieval, plant historian data, and reproducible
engineering reports.
