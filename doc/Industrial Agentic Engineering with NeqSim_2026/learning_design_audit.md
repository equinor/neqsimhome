The revised first chapter restores an actionable entry point and explicitly separates shell commands from host prompts. Chapter 7 develops that experience into a Standard study rather than repeating the whole installation guide. Chapters 9–11 have stated inputs, executed results, physical interpretation and application limits. The new conceptual scenes are labelled; their decorative content is not numerical evidence.

Learning boundaries: the book is a practical introduction, not a complete thermodynamics course, certified design procedure or demonstration of every advertised software capability. An instructor supplement with selected exercise self-checks would be useful, but it is not required to resolve the current revision.
